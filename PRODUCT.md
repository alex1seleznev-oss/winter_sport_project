# Winter Sports Hub — product context

Audience: Russian-speaking spectators of biathlon and cross-country skiing, viewing the international and Russian2026/27 seasons, often on a phone. Primary tasks: find a stage, see verified dates and race programme, distinguish qualification/final/cancelled entries, follow primary source and legitimate broadcast information. No requirement to create an account to read the calendar.

User-approved visual direction: retain dark premium winter-sports UI with icy-blue accents, editorial typography and stage cards. Real photographs only, original faces and competition equipment unchanged. No generated athlete likenesses and no copying dates, race counts or winner names from generated mockups. Photo captions must identify the actual prior-season date and source; athlete hero claims require verified results. Stage grids cover biathlon/international, biathlon/Russia, skiing/international, skiing/Russia.

Repository alex1seleznev-oss/winter_sport_project and Supabase wmiypacyraepljalppub only. Do not deploy into TopRunners. Public verified data overrides demonstrations. Official sources override media/social context. Programme publication and exact time are separate. Unknown time is not midnight. No paid services, public file upload or unreviewed auto-publisher enabled by a design release.
