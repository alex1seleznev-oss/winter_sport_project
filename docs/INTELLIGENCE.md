# Intelligence policy

Winter Sports Hub separates **fact**, **claim**, and **analysis**.

## Fact
Official schedule/result/start list from the governing body or organizer.

## Claim
A statement from an athlete, team, media outlet, community, fan page, or other public source. Claims remain unverified until corroborated.

## Analysis
A model-generated interpretation derived from stored facts and claims. Analysis always preserves evidence and confidence.

## Cross-source corroboration
- Tier 5 official source can confirm a factual schedule/result claim.
- Athlete/team first-party public statement can confirm what that person/team said, but not necessarily the underlying causal claim.
- Independent media/community sources increase confidence only when they are genuinely independent.
- Reposts do not count as independent corroboration.
- Fan pages are discovery sources and cannot alone create a confirmed fact.

## Learning
Source reliability is evaluated retrospectively by comparing prior claims with later confirmed outcomes. This adjusts source weighting; it never silently rewrites historical evidence.
